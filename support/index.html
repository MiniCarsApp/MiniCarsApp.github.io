Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with MiniCars? Fill in the form, and we will reply to your e-mail.

We use your name, e-mail and message only to answer you — see the Privacy Policy.

Or write directly to minicarsapp@proton.me. If something does not work, please mention your device and iOS version.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my collection stored?

Only on your device. To keep a copy, turn on iCloud Backup or export the collection in the app: Settings → Export to CSV.

A feature asks for access I declined earlier.

Open Settings → MiniCars on your device and turn the permission on. The app's own Settings have an “Open iOS Settings” button.

How does “Compare nearby” work?

Both people open “Compare nearby” in MiniCars with Bluetooth on. After one sends a request and the other accepts, the app shows which models from one wishlist are in the other collection. Only texts are sent — no photos or prices. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete models in the app, or delete the app to remove all its data from the device.
