Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Developer: MiniCars, the Netherlands

In short: the MiniCars app does not collect, store or share your personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer has no servers that receive data from the app — everything you add to it stays on your device.

1. Data you keep in the app

Models, photos, videos, voice notes and their transcripts, notes, tags, prices, purchase dates and places, and planned purchases are stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. The developer cannot see or access them.

If device backups are turned on (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), this data may be included in your backup. Backups are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy and your device settings.

2. Permissions

The app asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it. You can change any permission at any time in iOS Settings → MiniCars; the rest of the app keeps working.

PermissionWhat it is used for
CameraTaking photos and videos of your models.
MicrophoneSound in videos and voice notes.
Speech recognitionTurning voice notes into text. Recognition runs on your device only; audio is not sent anywhere.
PhotosAdding photos and videos from your library to a model.
Location (while using the app)Marking where you bought a model. To show the address, the coordinates are sent to Apple's map service.
ContactsChoosing a friend to tell about a new model. Contacts are read on your device and are not stored or sent by the app. You send the message yourself through Messages or Mail.
CalendarAdding an event for a planned purchase date through the system calendar screen. On iOS 17 and later no calendar access is requested.
Face ID, Touch ID or passcodeShowing hidden prices. The check is done by iOS; the app only receives the result.
Bluetooth“Compare nearby” with another collector (see section 3).
NotificationsReminders on a planned purchase day. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

3. “Compare nearby” over Bluetooth

“Compare nearby” lets two people standing next to each other compare a wishlist with a collection. It works directly between the two devices over Bluetooth, without the internet, and starts only after both people agree.

Only the following is sent to the other device: the name you choose for the comparison (up to 30 characters), a short code, and for each model in your wishlist or matching model in your collection — vehicle type, make, model, manufacturer, scale, colour, series and catalogue number. Photos, videos, voice notes, notes, tags, prices, dates and places are never sent. The other person's list is used only to show the result and is not saved after you close the screen.

4. Apple services

Maps and address lookup use Apple Maps; messages and e-mails are sent by you through Apple's Messages and Mail. These services are provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Export and sharing

Data leaves the app only when you decide so: for example, when you export your collection to a CSV file or share a message about a model through the system share sheet.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete any model with its photos, videos and notes in the app. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device.

7. Contacting support

When you write to us through the support form or by e-mail, we receive your name, e-mail address, the topic and your message. We use them only to answer you and do not share them with anyone for other purposes.

The form delivers the message through Web3Forms, and our mailbox is hosted by Proton Mail. We keep the correspondence as long as needed to help you and then delete it; you can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. Your rights

The developer is based in the Netherlands, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies. You have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you (in practice, only support correspondence), to restrict or object to its processing and to receive a copy of it. Write to minicarsapp@proton.me. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in your country.

9. Children

The MiniCars app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
